From Mathematics Through the Eyes of Faith

In Chapter 4 of Mathematics Through the Eyes of Faith (pp. 67–68), James Bradley and Russell Howell discuss this profound 1891 discovery by David Hilbert:

"Each stage scales down the prior motif by 1/4 in area, rotating the bottom-left by 90° CW, keeping top-left & top-right intact, and rotating bottom-right by 90° CCW, then connecting them into a single unbroken path."

Theological Significance: The sequence of continuous functions $f_n: [0, 1] \to [0, 1]^2$ converges uniformly to a limit curve $f$. Though 1-dimensional in topological dimension, its image completely fills the 2-dimensional square. Bradley and Howell invite contemplation on how finite mathematical models stretch human intuitions toward the infinite richness of God's creation.
